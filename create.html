Upload tips

  • Use equirectangular images with a 2:1 aspect ratio, such as 360° camera exports or renders from your 3D software.
  • JPG, PNG and WebP files work. Very large images are scaled down to 8K wide.
  • Name your files after the rooms. The file name becomes the scene name.
  • The first panorama is where the tour starts. You can reorder scenes in the editor.
