FAQ

Questions, answered

What kind of images can I use?

Any equirectangular panorama with a 2:1 aspect ratio, as JPG, PNG or WebP. Most 360° cameras export this format by default.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan lets you build and share tours with up to 150 scenes. PRO removes branding and adds custom domains, privacy controls and analytics.

Do viewers need an app?

No. Tours run in any modern browser on desktop, mobile and VR headsets.

Can I put a tour on my own website?

Every tour has an embed snippet you can paste into WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace or plain HTML.
