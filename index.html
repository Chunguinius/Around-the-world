FAQ

Questions, answered

What kind of images can I use?

Any equirectangular panorama with a 2:1 aspect ratio, as JPG, PNG or WebP. Most 360° cameras and render engines export this format.

Is it really free?

Yes. With a free account you can publish tours of up to 200 panoramas and share them with a link.

Do visitors need an app?

No. Tours run in any modern browser on computers, phones, tablets and headsets.

Can I put a tour on my own website?

Yes. Every published tour has an embed code you can paste into your site.
